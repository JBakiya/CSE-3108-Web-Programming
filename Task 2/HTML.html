Classic Margherita Pizza

A timeless Italian recipe from Naples

Prep: 20 min Cook: 12 min Serves: 4

About This Recipe

The Margherita is the king of Neapolitan pizza — simple ingredients, bold flavors. Named after Queen Margherita of Italy in 1889, it represents the colors of the Italian flag: red tomato, white mozzarella, and green basil.

Ingredients

500g bread flour (tipo 00)

7g active dry yeast

2 tsp salt

400g San Marzano tomatoes

Fresh basil leaves

325ml warm water

1 tsp sugar

2 tbsp olive oil

250g fresh mozzarella

Extra virgin olive oil for drizzling

Instructions

1

Make the dough: Dissolve yeast and sugar in warm water. Let sit for 5 minutes until foamy. Mix flour and salt, add yeast mixture and olive oil. Knead for 10 minutes until smooth and elastic.

2

First rise: Place dough in an oiled bowl, cover with a damp cloth. Let rise in a warm spot for 1–2 hours until doubled in size.

3

Preheat: Set your oven to the highest setting (250°C / 500°F). If you have a pizza stone, place it in the oven while preheating.

4

Shape the dough: Divide dough into 4 equal portions. On a floured surface, stretch each piece into a thin round using your hands (not a rolling pin — we want air pockets).

5

Top the pizza: Spread crushed San Marzano tomatoes evenly. Tear mozzarella into pieces and distribute over the sauce. Add a few basil leaves.

6

Bake: Slide onto the hot stone or baking sheet. Bake for 10–12 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling with brown spots.

7

Serve: Drizzle with extra virgin olive oil, add fresh basil, and serve immediately. A true Margherita waits for no one.

Tip: For the most authentic result, use tipo 00 flour and let your dough cold-ferment in the fridge for 24–72 hours. The longer fermentation develops deeper flavor and a better crust texture.
